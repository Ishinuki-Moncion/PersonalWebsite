About Me

I'm Ishinuki Daikie, a software developer and artist based in Tokyo by way of Dallas. I earned a B.S. in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas, then studied Japan Studies at Soka University in Tokyo. Today I'm a Technical Producer at SeenThis Japan, where I build and convert advertising materials into open-web digital ad banners with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, having carried 100+ campaigns from creation to execution.

I work across the stack — React, Next.js, Node, and PostgreSQL — and I speak native English and conversational Japanese (JLPT N3). Before SeenThis I contracted in front-end development and taught JavaScript to high-school students at All Star Code in NYC. Photography is a serious passion, and the gallery below is part of how I keep code and visual art in the same conversation.
